Here's to the next chapter.

The Beginning

I still remember the first time you came to my house. We were only 8, and somehow even then we already had completely different things going on. I was busy playing while you were sitting there studying. Neither of us could have possibly known that this random childhood visit would eventually turn into a friendship that would last for the next ten years.

The 2-Day Van Era 😂

Then came the legendary van phase. You joined my van, and somehow, only two days later, you left it again. 😂 At the time, it probably felt like just another completely normal school thing, but looking back, it is funny how many of our memories started with these tiny, seemingly insignificant moments. If someone had told us we'd still be friends ten years later, I definitely wouldn't have believed them.

Two Months of Awkwardness

At 9, we somehow ended up in the same class for about two months and even sat together. We were both awkward, probably had no idea what to say half the time, and definitely weren't the people we eventually became. Still, looking back, there's something really sweet about remembering those early days when our friendship was basically just beginning without either of us realizing it.

The Frenemy Era

By the time we were 10, things had evolved considerably. We entered what can only be described as our frenemy era. 😂 We could get along perfectly one moment and then argue over something completely ridiculous the next. Somehow, despite all the rivalry and stupid arguments, we kept ending up around each other. Apparently, even our childhood selves couldn't escape this friendship.

The Taekwondo Incident

One of the funniest things is that I once watched your taekwondo training while I was waiting for a friend, without even realizing that it was you. Imagine watching someone without knowing that years later they would become your best friend. 😂 It's such a random memory, but it makes me realize how many times our paths crossed before we actually became close.

Forced Proximity

At 11, I joined your van, and somehow we ended up being forced to sit together. Neither of us was particularly thrilled about this arrangement. 😂 We weren't exactly best friends at the time, and sitting next to each other probably felt like the universe was testing our patience. Looking back, though, it's hilarious because we eventually became so close that sitting together became completely normal.

Rivals, But Closer

Around 12, we started getting closer, although the rivalry definitely wasn't going anywhere. We could be friends and competitors at the same time, somehow managing to turn the smallest things into competitions or arguments. 😂 But underneath all that, we were slowly becoming more comfortable around each other. It was probably one of the first stages where our friendship started becoming what it is today.

Lockdown

Then lockdown happened, and somehow that changed everything. We became much closer during that time, especially when you were away in Aligarh for six months. I genuinely missed having you around, which was probably one of the first times I properly realized how important your presence had become. It's weird that something as strange as lockdown ended up strengthening our friendship so much.

The Instagram Era

At 14, we properly entered the Instagram era. 😂 We started talking more and more, and suddenly our conversations weren't just random interactions at school anymore. We could actually talk whenever we wanted. Slowly, the messages became longer, the conversations became more frequent, and we started knowing much more about each other's lives. Looking back, this was definitely one of the turning points in our friendship.

The Walk Home Era

By 15, we had become considerably closer, and going home from school together became one of those little routines that I genuinely enjoyed. Those walks gave us time to talk about random things, complain about school, discuss whatever was happening in our lives, and just exist without the usual school chaos around us. Some of the simplest moments ended up becoming some of my favourite memories.

The Water Incident 😂

And then there was the time you hit me because I got water on you. 😂 Honestly, I'm still not sure whether the punishment matched the crime. But somehow, even these ridiculous moments became part of what makes our friendship memorable. We've had plenty of arguments and stupid incidents over the years, but they're the kind of memories that we'll probably laugh about even years from now.

Slendrina 💀

I genuinely don't know why you thought pretending to be Slendrina was an acceptable way to entertain yourself, but apparently it was. 💀 You used to scare me with it, and I definitely did not appreciate it at the time. Looking back, though, it's one of those completely random memories that instantly reminds me of our friendship and the ridiculous things we used to do.

The Birthday Incident

You once invited me to your birthday, which I thought was going to be a nice, normal birthday celebration. Instead, somehow I ended up getting isolated. 😂 I still remember that because it was such a classic example of our friendship: something that should have been simple somehow turned into a completely memorable incident. At least now it's something we can laugh about.

Basketball 🏀

Playing basketball together at school is another one of those memories I really like. It wasn't necessarily some huge event, but it was just fun being able to play together and spend time doing something outside of the usual classroom routine. Looking back at all these little moments, it's funny how ordinary things like playing basketball can become memories that stick around for years.

The Wingwoman Era

One of the most important things you've done for me was helping me propose to my girlfriend. 😂 You were basically my unofficial wingwoman through the whole situation, and honestly, I probably would've overthought everything without your help. It's funny to think about how you've been involved in so many different parts of my life, sometimes in completely unexpected ways.

The Unofficial Therapist

You've also somehow become my unofficial therapist over the years. Whenever I had some problem, whether it was something serious or something completely stupid, you were usually there to listen and help me figure it out. You somehow had a way of making things feel less complicated. I genuinely appreciate all those conversations, even the ones where I probably made things way more dramatic than they needed to be.

The Chat Wars 😂

And obviously, we can't forget the countless fights we've had over chat. 😂 Sometimes we'd argue over something incredibly stupid, stop talking for a while, and then somehow end up talking again like nothing happened. I've probably had to make up for my fair share of those arguments too. Somehow, despite all the chaos, none of those fights managed to break the friendship.

Ten Years Later ❤️

And now here we are. Ten years after that first childhood visit, we've somehow gone from barely knowing each other, to classmates, rivals, frenemies, friends, and eventually best friends. We've grown up a lot, changed in countless ways, and collected an absurd number of memories along the way. Whatever the next ten years bring, I'm genuinely glad you've been such a big part of the first ten. ❤️
