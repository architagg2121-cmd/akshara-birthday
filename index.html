01

The Beginning

The first time you came to my house, I was playing while you were studying. We were only 8.

Neither of us knew that this random little visit would eventually become the beginning of a 10-year friendship.

02

The 2-Day Van Era 😂

You joined my van...

And then left two days later.

Honestly, a legendary start to our friendship.

03

Two Months of Awkwardness

We were 9 and ended up in the same class for two months. We even sat together.

It was awkward, but somehow still sweet.

04

The Frenemy Era

When we were 10, we discovered our true calling: being friends and rivals at exactly the same time.

05

The Taekwondo Incident

I once watched you during taekwondo training while waiting for my friend.

The funny part? I didn't even realize it was you at the time.

06

Forced Proximity

At 11, I joined your van and we were basically forced to sit together.

Neither of us particularly liked the situation. 😂

07

Rivals, But Closer

At 12, we started getting closer.

The rivalry wasn't going anywhere though.

08

Lockdown

Somehow, lockdown brought us closer.

And when you went to Aligarh for six months, I actually missed you.

09

The Instagram Era

At 14, we started properly chatting on Instagram.

Somewhere along the way, our friendship started becoming something much closer.

10

The Walk Home Era

At 15, we started going home from school together.

Those ordinary walks ended up becoming some of my favourite memories.

11

The Water Incident 😂

You hit me because I got water on you.

12

Slendrina 💀

You pretending to be Slendrina just to scare me was absolutely unnecessary.

13

You invited me to your birthday... and then proceeded to isolate me.

14

Basketball 🏀

Playing basketball together at school.

One of those simple memories that probably wouldn't seem important to anyone else, but somehow stuck with me.

15

The Wingwoman Era

16

Every time something went wrong, somehow I ended up talking to you about it.

17

The Chat Wars 😂

We've had our fair share of fights.

And somehow, after every argument, we'd eventually find our way back to each other.

18

Ten Years Later ❤️

And that's basically us.

From two kids who couldn't stand each other, to frenemies, rivals, classmates, van-mates, partners in crime, and eventually best friends.

Ten years is a ridiculously long time. We've changed, grown up, fought, laughed, annoyed each other, and made memories I wouldn't trade for anything.

I'm genuinely grateful that after all these years, you're still here.
